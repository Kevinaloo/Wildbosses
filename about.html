Wild Bosses was built on one belief: the best way to experience Kenya is through the people who call it home.

We run private and small-group tours, safaris and expeditions. Groups stay small because a full vehicle is a worse day out than a half-empty one — everyone gets a window, everyone hears the guide, and the driver can stop when something is worth stopping for.

Every departure on this site is a real date with real places on it. When a trip fills, it says full. We would rather show you four honest departures than forty that need a phone call to find out whether they exist.

Small groups

Local guides
