Our mission

Local knowledge
deserves full reward.

Most tour platforms take 20–30% commission from every booking. That means a guide who charges KES 10,000 for a full-day safari takes home KES 7,000 at best. We think that's wrong.

Wildbosses was built to flip this model. Guides list for free, set their own prices, and receive 100% of every booking directly to M-Pesa. We cover our costs through optional premium tools — never from cutting guides' pay.

The result? Guides earn more, prices stay fair, and travellers get the most authentic East Africa possible — not a corporate tour product, but a real human experience from someone who lives and breathes this land.

What we stand for

Six values.
No compromises.

💯
Zero commission
Guides keep 100% of every booking. Always. We will never take a cut from guide earnings — our business model doesn't depend on it.
🤝
Local first
We only list tours led by genuine local guides — people who were born in or have deep roots in the places they show. No corporate packages.
🛡️
Trust & safety
Every guide is background-checked, identity-verified and holds relevant certifications before their first listing goes live on Wildbosses.
🌍
Sustainability
We actively partner with conservation areas, community conservancies and ethical tourism operators. The wild must be protected to be experienced.
✨
Radical transparency
What you see is what you pay. No hidden fees, no booking surcharges, no currency conversion surprises. Prices are in KES and displayed clearly.
❤️
Community impact
When guides earn more, families thrive, schools get funded and communities grow. We measure our impact in livelihoods, not just bookings.

How we got here.

🌱
2021 · Nairobi
The frustration that started it all
Our founders watched a guide friend receive KES 6,500 from a booking that the platform charged KES 10,000 for. The 35% cut was neither disclosed to the guide nor the guest. Something had to change.
🔨
2022 · Building
First prototype — 12 guides, Nairobi only
We launched a simple WhatsApp-based booking system with 12 Nairobi walking guides. Within 3 months, 8 of them had doubled their monthly income. The model worked.
🚀
2023 · Expansion
Platform launched — Kenya-wide
Wildbosses.co went live with a full web platform, M-Pesa integration, and guides from Nairobi to the Masai Mara to Diani Beach. 120 guides. 340 tours. 1,800 bookings in year one.
🌍
2024 · East Africa
Tanzania, Uganda and Rwanda join
We expanded beyond Kenya — Serengeti guides, Zanzibar dhow captains, Ugandan gorilla tracking experts and Rwandan cultural ambassadors joined the platform. 280 guides. 800 tours.
⭐
2025 – Today
380+ guides · 1,200+ tours · 60+ countries of guests
Wildbosses has processed over KES 180 million in guide earnings since launch — with zero commission deducted. Our guides earn an average of KES 42,000/month. We're just getting started.
