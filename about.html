Our story

Born from a
deep love of this land.

Wildbosses was founded in Nairobi in 2013 by a team of local guides, naturalists and storytellers who believed that tourism could be more personal, more honest and more meaningful.

We started with a single city walking tour of Nairobi CBD. Within two years we had added Masai Mara game drives, Kilimanjaro climbs and Zanzibar cultural expeditions. Today we operate across Kenya and Tanzania with a team of 12 specialist guides — each a world-class expert in their field.

What hasn't changed: we still run every tour personally. No subcontracting. No third parties. When you book Wildbosses, you get Wildbosses.

What we stand for

Our principles,
not just our policies.

🌍
Depth over breadth
We run fewer tours than most companies. Each one is carefully designed and personally led. We'd rather do a small number of things brilliantly than a large number of things adequately.
🤝
Genuinely local
Every Wildbosses guide was born and raised in the region they lead. No outsiders reading scripts. You get authentic knowledge, real stories and genuine connections.
🦁
Wildlife first
We operate strict vehicle limits and off-road policies. Your experience should never come at the expense of the animals. We train every guide in ethical wildlife interaction.
💚
Community benefit
5% of every booking goes directly to the Wildbosses Community Fund — supporting conservation education, anti-poaching rangers and wildlife corridor preservation across Kenya.
🔍
Radical transparency
Every price on our website is the price you pay. No hidden fees, no "park fee supplements", no last-minute additions. What you see is what you pay.
⭐
Small groups, always
Our safaris cap at 6 guests per vehicle. Our city walks cap at 16. If a tour is full, it's full — we don't squeeze in extra people to earn more. Your experience matters more than our revenue.
Our journey

Twelve years of
remarkable mornings.

2013
First steps in Nairobi
Founded with a single Nairobi walking tour. First month: 8 guests. First review: 5 stars. James Mwangi, our founding guide, still leads every city walk personally.
2015
Into the Mara
Launched Masai Mara safaris with our first private Land Cruiser. Grace Wanjiku joined as safari director. Our first year: 64 guests. Average rating: 4.94.
2017
Roof of Africa
Added Kilimanjaro climbs under Felix Massawe, our mountain director. First season: 12 successful summits from 13 attempts — a remarkable debut success rate.
2019
Zanzibar & the Coast
Expanded to Tanzania with Ali Hassan leading Stone Town tours, and Omar Salim bringing Diani dhow experiences. Now a true East Africa operation.
2021
Rebuilding after COVID
With the world closed, we used 2020–21 to retrain, recertify, and rebuild our fleet. We returned stronger, with every guide holding new wildlife & first aid certifications.
2024
1,000th tour milestone
Completed our 1,000th Wildbosses tour. Average rating across all: 4.97 / 5. 847 five-star reviews on Google. Featured in Lonely Planet's Best of Kenya guide.
2026
Today — and tomorrow
Over 1,200 happy travellers and growing. We remain committed to small group sizes, personal guiding, and the belief that the best adventures are the ones you didn't expect.
